Tiếng ViệtTiếng Việt Dojo: grammar and conversation practice

Study any other grammar point

Learn the words and conversations of one field. Choose a theme and a level.

Pick a situation. Your teacher, Cô giáo, plays the other person at the level you choose, corrects every message you send, and suggests what to say next.


Hands-free: she speaks, you answer aloud, and she replies when you pause.

Settings

The app works without a key. If you fill one in, it is used instead and saved only in this browser.
Microphone not working? Browsers often block it for files opened by double-click. In the file's folder run python -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000/tiengviet-dojo.html.
